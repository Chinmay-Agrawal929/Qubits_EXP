What am I seeing?

The Bloch sphere is a geometric picture of a single qubit. For an n-qubit system each selected qubit is shown through its reduced density matrix (partial trace over all other qubits), so you see the local state of that qubit while the full state remains a 2n-dimensional vector.

• Surface → pure qubit state
• Inside → mixed reduced state
• Center → maximally mixed qubit

Qubit order: Q0 is the leftmost bit of a basis label.
